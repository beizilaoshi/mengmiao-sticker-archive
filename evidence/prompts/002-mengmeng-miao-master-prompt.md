# Main Prompt — Mengmeng Miao

**Character Name: Mengmeng Miao (筑梦喵)**

A ridiculously adorable anime catgirl mascot with an energetic, cheerful, affectionate personality. A beautifully detailed Japanese anime illustration with soft watercolor textures, delicate hand-drawn linework, pastel colors, and a dreamy, heartwarming atmosphere.

## 1. Character Appearance

A cute young anime catgirl with a small, delicate body, an oversized adorable head, soft rounded facial features, and a charming chibi-inspired appearance.

- Extremely long, fluffy, voluminous hair.
- Pure white, silver-white, and pale lavender-purple hair colors.
- Soft lavender gradients throughout the hair.
- Beautiful layered hair strands with delicate individual highlights.
- A distinctive large upward-curving ahoge (single strand of hair) on top of her head, forming an elegant spiral-like curl.
- Two large fluffy cat ears emerging naturally from her hair.
- Outer cat ears are white with subtle lavender shading.
- Inner ears are soft pastel pink with delicate gradients.
- Soft, fluffy ear fur with carefully illustrated individual strands.

## 2. Eyes and Facial Features

- Large, sparkling heterochromatic anime eyes.
- Left eye: warm golden amber, honey-yellow iris with intricate radial details.
- Right eye: brilliant sapphire blue with layered blue gradients.
- Both eyes feature multiple glossy highlights and intricate reflections.
- Long, delicate upper eyelashes with dark purple outlines.
- One eye playfully closed in a cheerful wink.
- The other eye wide open, sparkling with excitement.
- Small, delicate nose.
- Wide-open happy mouth with a soft pink interior.
- Blushing cheeks with beautiful pastel pink shading.
- A joyful, affectionate expression.
- Extremely cute, welcoming, playful facial expression.

## 3. Outfit and Clothing

A beautiful traditional-inspired fantasy outfit combining Japanese shrine maiden aesthetics with elegant winter clothing.

- Main clothing colors: sapphire blue, pure white, and pale lavender.
- A luxurious white fluffy kimono-inspired top.
- Thick, soft white fur trim around the sleeves and collar.
- Large oversized sleeves with fluffy cuffs.
- Delicate blue fabric borders and decorative stitching.
- A prominent sapphire-blue ribbon around the chest.
- A beautiful golden bell attached to the center of the ribbon.
- Small dangling golden ornaments beneath the bell.
- A blue waist ribbon tied into a decorative bow.
- Delicate white cherry blossom decorations attached to the outfit.
- Intricate floral embroidery and tiny decorative details.
- Soft, layered white and blue fabric with elegant folds.

## 4. Hair Accessories and Decorations

- Large sapphire-blue fabric bows attached near both cat ears.
- Multiple delicate white cherry blossom ornaments.
- Cherry blossoms with pale yellow centers.
- Small golden bells hanging from decorative ribbons.
- Golden braided tassels with diamond-shaped ornaments.
- Tiny blue crystal decorations.
- Delicate ornamental details with a refined fantasy aesthetic.
- All accessories should be symmetrical in overall design while retaining natural artistic variation.

## 5. Pose and Body Language

The character is facing directly toward the viewer.

- Both arms extended forward toward the camera.
- Both hands open with palms facing the viewer.
- Fingers naturally spread apart in a welcoming gesture.
- Hands are clearly human hands, with five anatomically correct fingers on each hand.
- Arms slightly foreshortened toward the camera.
- Oversized fluffy sleeves framing both hands.
- Upper body leaning slightly forward.
- The character appears to be enthusiastically welcoming or hugging the viewer.
- Her posture conveys excitement, affection, friendliness, and happiness.

The pose should feel spontaneous, lively, and irresistibly adorable.

## 6. Composition

- Perfect square 1:1 aspect ratio.
- Close-up to medium-close-up character portrait.
- Character occupies approximately 90% of the entire canvas.
- Head and upper body dominate the composition.
- Cat ears and hair decorations remain fully visible.
- Both extended hands are visible near the lower left and lower right sides.
- Face positioned near the center.
- Slight perspective exaggeration to emphasize the welcoming gesture.
- Balanced composition with no awkward cropping.
- Character is the absolute focal point.

## 7. Background and Decorative Elements

- Clean, pure white background.
- Minimalist composition without environmental scenery.
- Two or three soft pastel pink heart doodles floating near the upper-right side.
- Three small pink excitement marks near the upper-left side.
- Hand-drawn decorative symbols.
- Soft, subtle pink accents.
- No distracting background elements.
- Plenty of visual clarity around the character.

## 8. Lighting and Color Palette

- Bright, soft, high-key lighting.
- Gentle ambient illumination.
- Delicate pastel color transitions.
- Soft lavender and pale blue shadows.
- Warm pink skin tones.
- Subtle golden highlights on bells and ornaments.
- Beautiful glossy highlights in the eyes.
- Soft ambient occlusion around hair and clothing folds.
- No harsh shadows.
- No excessive contrast.

## 9. Illustration Style and Rendering

- Premium Japanese anime character illustration.
- Delicate hand-drawn watercolor aesthetic.
- Soft colored-pencil and watercolor brush textures.
- Fine, expressive sketch-like outlines.
- Beautifully layered hair rendering.
- Detailed individual hair strands.
- Intricate fabric folds and embroidery.
- Soft painterly shading.
- Delicate pastel gradients.
- Beautifully rendered fluffy fur.
- High-quality anime facial rendering.
- Extremely detailed eyes.
- Subtle artistic imperfections that preserve the hand-painted appearance.
- Dreamy, ethereal, cute, and heartwarming atmosphere.
- Professional anime character illustration quality.

## 10. Overall Impression

The final illustration should make Mengmeng Miao look like an adorable anime mascot enthusiastically reaching out to hug the viewer.

She should feel warm, lovable, lively, playful, and emotionally expressive.

The artwork should closely resemble a beautifully hand-painted Japanese anime character illustration, with a soft pastel watercolor aesthetic, intricate details, and an irresistibly cute personality.

**Character identity must remain consistent: white and lavender long hair, heterochromatic golden and blue eyes, fluffy cat ears, sapphire-blue ribbons, golden bells, and delicate cherry blossom ornaments.**
